# CHECKPOINT 002 — SPECIMEN FORMATION CONTRACT

Status: EXPERIMENTAL

Architecture: NOT FROZEN
Inspector: NOT ESTABLISHED
Implementation: NONE

UNKNOWN -> HOLD

## Purpose

Form three synthetic specimens capable of distinguishing:

1. legitimate composition coverage;
2. unexplained material authority delta;
3. technical composition without material authority delta.

The specimens describe facts to be inspected later.

They do not determine their own outcomes.

## Formation Rule

Specimen facts
!=
inspection findings

Expected result
!=
observed result

Declared authority
!=
verified authority

Declared capability
!=
verified capability

Declared composition
!=
verified composition

The later experiment must independently inspect each relevant property.

## Required Specimen Surfaces

Each specimen must separately identify:

- specimen identity;
- component capability A;
- component capability B;
- component authority evidence;
- proposed composition;
- resulting capability;
- candidate material delta;
- candidate authority coverage;
- expected control role.

No field may silently substitute for another.

## Specimen A

Role:
POSITIVE CONTROL

Purpose:
Demonstrate that an explicit competent grant may legitimately cover composition.

Required condition:

Authority evidence must explicitly include:

- Capability A;
- Capability B;
- the defined composition;
- the resulting consequential reach.

The experiment must be capable of returning:

COMPOSITION AUTHORITY -> PASS

without requiring a second grant merely because composition occurred.

## Specimen B

Role:
PRIMARY NEGATIVE CONTROL

Purpose:
Expose potential Unexplained Authority Delta.

Required condition:

- Capability A individually covered.
- Capability B individually covered.
- Composition technically defined.
- Resulting capability introduces material consequential reach.
- Provided authority evidence does not explicitly establish coverage of that additional reach.

Required epistemic posture:

COMPOSITION AUTHORITY -> HOLD

unless later inspection discovers competent coverage.

The specimen itself must not encode "unauthorized."

## Specimen C

Role:
NO-MATERIAL-DELTA CONTROL

Purpose:
Ensure composition alone does not trigger authority expansion.

Required condition:

- Capability A covered.
- Capability B covered.
- Composition exists.
- Resulting operation remains inside already established reach.
- No new target, persistence, binding force, external consequence, or scope is introduced.

Expected candidate finding:

MATERIAL DELTA -> NOT_ESTABLISHED

## Prohibition

No specimen may contain its final inspection result as an authoritative field.

Expected outcomes may exist only as test-oracle documentation.
