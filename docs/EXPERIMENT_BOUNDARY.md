# EXPERIMENT BOUNDARY

## Purpose

Test one bounded question:

Can independently authorized capabilities compose into a capability whose additional reach is not independently explained by the authority supporting its components?

## Variables To Keep Separate

Capability B
Authority A

Composition

Authority Delta

## Core Separations

Capability != Authority

Component Authority != Composition Authority

Executable != Authorized

Individually Authorized != Collectively Authorized

New Capability != New Authority

Authority Persistence != Authority Expansion

Authority Inheritance != Authority Composition

Missing Authority Evidence != Proven Absence Of Authority

## Hold Rule

If competent authority for the composed reach cannot be established:

COMPOSITION AUTHORITY -> HOLD

Do not silently inherit authority from the component capabilities.

## Scope

No operational enforcement.

No real-world permissions.

No production systems.

No person ranking.

No automatic authority determination.

Synthetic specimens only for the first experiment.
